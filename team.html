Founder · Engineer · Composer

Murray Anderson

Murray Anderson started Milestone Studios over 35 years ago. He has recorded many albums which have earned him Gold and Platinum records, has received awards for recording the Best Rock Album of the Year (for Robin Auld) and Best Jazz Album of the Year (for Abdullah Ibrahim), and was the first winner of the Professional Sound Engineer category of the Vuka Awards in 2000.

He has engineered recordings at Milestone Studios for local artists ranging from Amampondo to Jack Parow, HHP, Yvonne Chaka Chaka and the Klipwerf Orkes, jazz artists like Robbie Jansen, Bokani Dyer and Mandla Mlangeni, and international acts including Annie Lennox, Kaiser Chiefs, Junkie XL, Miley Cyrus and Queen. He recorded and produced Mandisi Dyantyis' debut album Somandla in 2018.

A prolific jingle writer, Murray has also scored the music for local and overseas films and TV programmes, including Country of My Skull, A Boy Called Twist, Hopeville, Krotoa and Felix. In 2005, he and Warrick Sony won the "Best Music" Gemini Award, one of Canada's highest accolades, for composing the music in Madiba: The Life and Times of Nelson Mandela. He won a SAFTA for Best Achievement in Original Music/Score for the TV series Isikizi, and has scored over 1,000 episodes of Arendsvlei.

Engineer · Studio Manager

Claire Anderson

Claire Anderson is one of the engineers, and oversees the day-to-day running of the studio. She received two degrees from UCT: a BA in English Literature and Historical Studies, and a BA Hons in Historical Studies. Before working as an engineer, Claire spent several years as a freelance editor and proofreader.

In addition to her managerial role, Claire has been responsible for Milestone's voice-over work, aided by a keen ear and her training in textual analysis. Locally, she has worked on advertising campaigns for everyone from TOPS at SPAR to Colgate and back again. She has recorded ADR for clients like Netflix, Lucasfilm, Warner Bros, Constantin Film and Disney, and produced podcasts and audiobooks for local and international clients. Beyond voice-over work, she enjoys assisting with music recordings in the live room.

Senior Engineer

Mathew Thiem

Mathew Thiem joined the Milestone team in 2023 and is a senior engineer. He graduated from Cape Audio College, and has done a variety of live and studio work since then: everything from being the sound technician for Jeremy Loops to monitoring live for Mi Casa and Majozi at Concerts in the Park.

Since joining Milestone, Mathew has worked on recordings for a wide range of bands, choirs, orchestras and solo artists, including Zolani Mahola, Moodymann, Kesivan Naidoo, Callum Kerr, Kaytranada, Odeal and Elijah Fox. He has also mixed many artists in Dolby Atmos, including Nasty C, Ryan Ofei, Mpho Wav, Khaid, Ronis Goliath and Gemma Fassie.
