FAQ

Good
questions

Does it cost anything to apply or join?

No. There are no application fees, and you never pay to be on the label. Studio costs are recouped only from your earnings on releases and gigs we book.

Do I keep the rights to my music?

Yes. You keep your masters and your publishing. First Milestone is a development deal, not a lifetime contract. We take an agreed commission on bookings and releases while you're with us.

How long does the programme last?

Typically 12 to 24 months, depending on how quickly you build momentum. When you're ready to move on, we help you do it.

I'm still at school. Can I apply?

Yes, if you're in Grade 10, 11 or 12. Your parent or guardian needs to be part of the conversation from the start and signs any agreement with you. Sessions are scheduled after school or in the holidays, never in school time.

I'm not in Cape Town. Can I still apply?

Yes. Sessions happen at our Cape Town studio, and we can help plan recording blocks around your school or university holidays.

What happens when someone wants to book me?

They enquire through this website or directly with us. We check your availability, negotiate the fee and paperwork, and you focus on the show.
