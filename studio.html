Our story

Four tracks
to five studios

1987

A cassette four-track in Woodstock

Murray Anderson, Patrick Lee Thorp and Zayn Adam start recording demos for Mountain Records songwriters in the Old Castle Brewery.

1990s

Bloem Street

The studio moves to central Cape Town and grows to three Pro Tools rooms with a live room for bands and choirs. Murray Stewart joins as partner in 1993.

2005

A Gemini Award

Murray and Warrick Sony win Canada's Gemini Award for Best Music for Madiba: The Life and Times of Nelson Mandela.

2007

The new complex

Three years of construction deliver Ivan Kadey's room-within-a-room design and the Waterland FrontWall control room.

2027

First Milestone

We launch a developmental label to give first-time school and university artists their start.
